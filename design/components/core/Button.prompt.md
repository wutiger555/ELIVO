Action button; capsule-shaped; primary (Ion fill) is reserved for the single most important action in a view.
```jsx
<Button variant="primary" icon="play">開始會議</Button>
<Button>看依據</Button>
<Button variant="ghost" size="sm">略過</Button>
```
- variants: primary · secondary · ghost · danger. sizes: sm 30 / md 38 / lg 50. secondary = liquid glass.
- Press = spring scale(.96). Labels are short verbs; no trailing punctuation.
